How long are leftovers good for in the fridge?

Published November 26, 2026 · Updated November 26, 2026

Short answer

Most cooked leftovers — meat, chicken, fish, soups, chili, casseroles, and pizza — keep 3 to 4 days in the refrigerator; cooked rice and pasta or potato salads keep 3 to 5 days[1]. Refrigerate within 2 hours of cooking (1 hour above 90°F) and reheat to 165°F[2]. Smell and appearance aren’t reliable tests[3].

Refrigerator times for common leftovers

LeftoverRefrigeratorFreezer (best quality)

If you won’t eat a leftover within those few days, freeze it while it’s fresh rather than on day four.

The rules that keep leftovers safe

  • Get food into the fridge within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if it’s been above 90°F[2].
  • Keep the refrigerator at 40°F or below and the freezer at 0°F or below[2].
  • Divide big batches into smaller, shallow containers so they cool quickly[1][3].
  • Label leftovers with the date so you know when they were made[3].
  • Reheat leftovers to 165°F, checking with a food thermometer[2].
  • Reheat only the portion you plan to eat, and leave the rest in the fridge[1].
  • Thaw frozen leftovers in the refrigerator, in cold water, or in the microwave[2].

Common questions, by food

  • Pizza: 3–4 days in the fridge[1]. Store slices in a sealed container, not the box.
  • Rice: 3–5 days[1]. Cool it quickly by spreading it out in a shallow container, then refrigerate.
  • Soup and chili: 3–4 days[1]. Divide big pots into smaller containers before refrigerating[3].
  • Roast chicken: 3–4 days[1]. Pull the meat off the bones so it cools and stores more easily.
  • Pasta salad or potato salad: 3–5 days[1]. Keep it cold and don’t leave it out at picnics for more than 2 hours[2].

Feeding leftovers to young children

The same rules apply to kids’ food. Serve a child’s portion onto a separate plate rather than feeding directly from the storage container, and throw away what’s left on their plate rather than saving it. If you have questions about storing baby food or formula, ask your pediatrician.

Why you can’t trust the sniff test

Mold and odors aren’t reliable ways to tell whether food has gone bad[3]. Go by the date and how the food was stored, and when in doubt, throw it out.

More quick answers

  • What fridge temperature keeps leftovers safe? Keep the refrigerator at 40°F or below and the freezer at 0°F or below[2]. A simple fridge thermometer lets you check.
  • Is it safe to put hot food straight in the fridge? Yes — get it in within 2 hours[2], but divide large amounts into smaller containers first so they cool quickly[1].

Leftovers are a meal-planning tool, not an afterthought. Cooking once and eating twice saves time and money, as long as the second meal falls inside the safe window.

Frequently asked questions

Are leftovers still good after 5 days?

For most cooked meats, soups, and casseroles, no — the guideline is 3 to 4 days[1]. Cooked rice and pasta or potato salads can go 3 to 5 days[1]. Freeze anything you won’t eat in time.

Can I reheat leftovers more than once?

It’s better not to. Reheat only the portion you’re going to eat and keep the rest refrigerated[1].

How long can leftovers sit out before going in the fridge?

No more than 2 hours, or 1 hour if the temperature is above 90°F[2].

Are frozen leftovers safe forever?

Frozen food stays safe for much longer than refrigerated food[1], but quality drops over time. For best taste, eat most frozen leftovers within two to three months[3].

How do I keep track of leftovers?

Label each container with the date[3] and keep leftovers on one shelf at eye level. Plan a leftover night midweek so they get eaten in time.
