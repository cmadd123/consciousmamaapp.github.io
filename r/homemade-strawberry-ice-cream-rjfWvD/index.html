Homemade Strawberry Ice Cream

Adapted from alattefood.com

Prep40 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups strawberries, hulled and diced
  • 2 Tbsp honey
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 cup heavy whipping cream
  • 1/2 cup half and half
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. In a big bowl, combine strawberries, honey, sugar, and lemon juice. Let it sit for 15-20 minutes to release the juices.
  2. Use a potato masher, fork, or pastry cutter to mash the mixture until no large strawberry pieces remain. For a smoother ice cream, blend it.
  3. Add heavy cream, half and half, and vanilla extract to the strawberries. Stir gently until everything is mixed well.
  4. Transfer the mixture to your ice cream maker and follow the instructions. It should churn for about 15-20 minutes. Enjoy it soft-serve, or freeze in an airtight container for 3-4 hours to thicken. Let it sit for 5-10 minutes at room temperature before serving.
  5. Dig in!
