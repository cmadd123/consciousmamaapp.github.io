Best Grilled Chicken Marinade Recipe

Adapted from mettehelbak.com

Prep10 min
Cook16 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons honey
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, mix together olive oil, soy sauce, honey, lemon juice, minced garlic, ground cumin, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper until blended well.
  2. Put the boneless, skinless chicken breasts in a resealable bag or shallow dish. Pour the marinade over the chicken, making sure every piece is coated. Seal or cover.
  3. Let the chicken marinate in the fridge for at least 30 minutes, or up to 8 hours for richer flavor and tenderness.
  4. Preheat the grill to medium-high for even cooking and those nice grill marks.
  5. Take the chicken out of the marinade and place it on the grill. Cook for 6 to 8 minutes on each side, flipping once, until it reaches 165°F (74°C).
  6. After cooking, let the chicken rest for a few minutes to keep it juicy and flavorful before serving.
