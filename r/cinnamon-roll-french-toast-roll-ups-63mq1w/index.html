Cinnamon Roll French Toast Roll-Ups

Adapted from recipesicon.com

Prep15 min
Cook10 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 10 slices white sandwich bread
  • 3 tablespoons butter (melted)
  • 3 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 2 tablespoons ground cinnamon
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1 tablespoon milk
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Cut off the crusts from the bread and flatten each slice with a rolling pin.
  2. In a small bowl, combine melted butter, brown sugar, and cinnamon for the filling.
  3. Spread the filling on each flattened slice, then roll them up tightly and seal the edges.
  4. In a shallow bowl, whisk together eggs, milk, cinnamon, and vanilla until smooth.
  5. Heat a skillet on medium and melt some butter. Dip each roll in the egg mixture and place seam-side down in the skillet.
  6. Cook for about 2-3 minutes, turning to brown all sides evenly.
