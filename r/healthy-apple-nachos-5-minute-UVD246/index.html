HEALTHY APPLE NACHOS (5-MINUTE)

Adapted from simple-veganista.com

Prep5 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 2 apples of choice
  • 1/4 &#8211; 1/3 cup natural nut butter (peanut, almond, sunflower, etc.)
  • small handful chocolate chips (I used Enjoy Life Mini Chips)
  • small handful shredded coconut
  • small handful slivered almonds, optional
  • sprinkle of cinnamon
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • hemp hearts
  • cacao nibs
  • raisins or currants
  • ground cardamom
  • pure maple syrup

Instructions

  1. Start by washing, coring, and slicing your apples into 1/4 inch pieces. Feel free to adjust thickness to your liking! Toss apple slices in a bowl with lemon juice.
  2. Gently warm your nut butter until it's drizzly. You can do this in a small silicone bowl over simmering water or in the microwave for 20-30 seconds.
  3. On a serving plate, arrange the apple slices in a single layer around the edge, then add another layer towards the center. Drizzle the nut butter in a circular motion or zigzag it across the apples. Top with chocolate chips, coconut, almonds, and a sprinkle of cinnamon.
  4. This recipe serves one generously or two as a small snack.
  5. Enjoy this quick and tasty treat!
