The BEST Oreo Dessert

Adapted from fantabulosity.com

Prep20 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 1 package Double Stuf Oreos (regular or family size)
  • 1/2 cup melted butter
  • 2 - 3.9 oz. packages of instant chocolate pudding mix
  • 3 ¼ cups milk
  • 2 - 8 ounces containers Cool Whip (thawed - see expert tips in blog post)
  • 8 ounces cream cheese (softened to room temperature)
  • 3/4 cup powdered sugar

Instructions

  1. Take 2 rows of double stuffed Oreos and put them in a large Ziploc bag. Seal it and crush the cookies into small pieces using your hands. A rolling pin or food processor works too!
  2. Pour the crushed Oreos into a 9 x 13-inch baking pan.
  3. Crush the last row of Oreos in the Ziploc bag for the topping.
  4. Drizzle the melted butter over the Oreo chunks in the pan and mix well. Press down and pop it in the fridge to chill.
  5. In a large bowl, combine milk and pudding powder using a stand mixer (or hand mixer). Start on low, then increase speed until thick.
  6. Pour the pudding into a bowl and chill in the fridge. Clean the mixing bowl and whip the softened cream cheese until fluffy.
  7. Gradually mix in the powdered sugar with the cream cheese until smooth. Gently fold in one container of Cool Whip.
  8. Take the Oreo crust from the fridge and layer the dessert: Oreo crust, cream cheese mixture, chocolate pudding, Cool Whip, and Oreo crumbs.
  9. Cover the dessert with plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 2 hours before slicing.
