30-Minute Ground Beef Stroganoff

Adapted from cookingupmemories.com

Cook30 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 6 ounces Egg Noodles
  • 1 pound Ground Beef
  • ¼ cup Butter
  • ¼ cup Flour
  • 1 cup Beef Broth
  • 1 ¼ cup Milk
  • ½ teaspoon Garlic Powder
  • ½ teaspoon Onion Powder
  • 1 teaspoon Pepper
  • 1 teaspoon Salt
  • ¼ cup Sour Cream

Instructions

  1. Start by boiling a pot of water and cook the noodles as per the package instructions. Set them aside when done.
  2. While the noodles are cooking, heat a large skillet on medium. Add the ground beef, breaking it apart with a spoon, and cook until browned, about 6-8 minutes. Drain excess fat and place the beef on a plate.
  3. In the same skillet, melt the butter over medium heat. Once it's melted, add the flour and whisk for about 2 minutes to make a roux.
  4. Slowly add the beef broth to the roux while whisking to keep it smooth. Keep whisking until the sauce thickens, around 3-4 minutes.
  5. Next, whisk in the milk, stirring over medium heat until the sauce thickens again, about 5-7 minutes. It should be creamy and smooth.
  6. Mix in the garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, and salt, ensuring everything is well combined in the sauce.
  7. Lower the heat to low. Add the cooked egg noodles and browned beef to the skillet, stirring until everything is heated through.
  8. Gently mix in the sour cream until it’s fully blended into the sauce, adding a nice tangy flavor.
  9. Serve hot with your favorite veggies or a slice of garlic toast for a complete meal.
