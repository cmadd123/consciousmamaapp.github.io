Smashburger Quesadillas

Adapted from minikitchenmagic.com

Prep10 min
Cook10 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • Salt, pepper, garlic powder (to season)
  • 4 large flour tortillas
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar or American cheese
  • Optional: pickles, diced onions, burger sauce for serving

Instructions

  1. Warm up a skillet or griddle on medium-high heat.
  2. Place a small handful of ground beef on one half of each tortilla. Don’t forget to season!
  3. Cook the beef side down for about 2–3 minutes until it’s nicely browned.
  4. Flip it over, add cheese, fold the tortilla, and grill until both sides are crispy and the cheese is melted.
  5. Cut into slices and enjoy with your favorite burger toppings or dipping sauce.
