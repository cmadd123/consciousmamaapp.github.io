Vanilla Granola (gluten-free, dairy free, vegan option)

Adapted from milehighmitts.com

Prep10 min
Cook30 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 2 cups rolled oats (certified gluten-free)
  • 2 cups chopped nuts (walnuts, almonds or pecans)
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/4 cup coconut oil
  • 1/3 cup honey (or maple syrup, if vegan)
  • 1 tbsp vanilla

Instructions

  1. Start by preheating your oven to 300°F and lining a baking pan with parchment paper.
  2. In a big bowl, combine your dry ingredients: oats, nuts, and cinnamon. Mix well.
  3. Melt your coconut oil in a microwave-safe bowl for about a minute. Stir in the honey, then warm it again for 30-60 seconds. Add the vanilla and mix.
  4. Pour the wet mixture over the dry ingredients and stir until everything is well coated. Spread the granola evenly on the pan. Bake for 25-30 minutes until the edges start to brown. It will crisp up as it cools.
  5. Allow the granola to cool completely before breaking it into pieces. Enjoy!
