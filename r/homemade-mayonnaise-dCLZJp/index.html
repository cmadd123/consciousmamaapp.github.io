Homemade Mayonnaise

Adapted from tenacrebaker.com

Prep5 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 2 large eggs (farm fresh is ideal)
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice ((I use an entire small lemon))
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp distilled vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp ground mustard
  • 2 cups Extra light olive oil (be sure to use light)

Instructions

  1. In a wide mouth mason jar, combine eggs, lemon juice, salt, vinegar, and ground mustard. Blend with an immersion blender until creamy and well mixed.
  2. While blending, slowly drizzle in the oil, moving the blender around for even mixing. Taste and adjust with more lemon, salt, or mustard if needed.
  3. Transfer the mayonnaise to an airtight container and store it in the refrigerator.
