The Best Homemade Vanilla Ice Cream EVER

Adapted from passingdownthelove.com

Prep10 min
Cook45 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 2 Cups Heavy Cream
  • 2 Cups Whole Milk
  • Pinch of Salt
  • 3/4 Cups Sugar
  • 2 Tbls. Vanilla Extract

Instructions

  1. Combine all ingredients and pour into your ice cream maker container.
  2. Feel free to mix in fruits, chocolate, chips, or nuts if you like.
  3. Follow the instructions for your ice cream maker to churn.
  4. This recipe yields 1 quart.
  5. You can easily double or triple the recipe to make more.
  6. Remember, ice cream maker sizes can differ, so adjust as needed.
  7. Personalize this recipe to make it your own!
