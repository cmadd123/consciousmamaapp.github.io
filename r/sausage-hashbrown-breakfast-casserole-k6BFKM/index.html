Sausage Hashbrown Breakfast Casserole

Adapted from saltysidedish.com

Prep5 min
Cook60 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 8 eggs
  • 1 pound pork sausage (jimmy dean)
  • 1 1/2 cups whole milk or heavy cream
  • 1 package 30 oz bag frozen shredded hash browns (thawed out)
  • 2 cups cheddar cheese
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1/4 tsp garlic powder

Instructions

  1. Set your oven to 375°F and lightly coat a 9 x 13 pan with non-stick spray.
  2. In a skillet over medium heat, cook the pork sausage until browned, breaking it into small pieces. Drain the grease and set aside.
  3. In a bowl, whisk together the eggs, heavy cream, and all the spices.
  4. Begin layering in the casserole dish: start with thawed hash browns, then add the cooked sausage, a layer of cheese, and finally pour the egg mixture on top.
  5. Cover the dish with foil.
  6. Bake for 40 minutes, then remove the foil and continue baking for an additional 20 minutes.
  7. Enjoy your Sausage Hashbrown Breakfast Casserole with some Frank's Hot Sauce or chunky salsa.
