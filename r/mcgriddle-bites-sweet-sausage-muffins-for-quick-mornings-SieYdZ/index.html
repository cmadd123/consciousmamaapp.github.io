McGriddle Bites: Sweet Sausage Muffins for Quick Mornings

Adapted from creamycomfortfood.com

Prep10 min
Cook15 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 1 pound breakfast sausage (use regular pork sausage)
  • 2 cups Hungry Jack Pancake Mix (make sure it only requires water)
  • 1 cup water (filtered or tap water)
  • 1/4 cup maple syrup (pure maple syrup preferred)
  • 1 cup cheddar cheese (shredded from block cheese)
  • 1/4 cup diced jalapeños (adds a spicy kick)
  • 1/2 cup blueberries (for a fruity contrast)

Instructions

  1. Set your oven to 400ºF (204ºC) and lightly grease a mini muffin tin. Cook the breakfast sausage in a skillet until it's browned and crumbled, about 5-7 minutes. Drain any extra grease. In a large bowl, mix the pancake mix, water, and maple syrup until just combined. Gently fold in the cooked sausage and shredded cheddar cheese. Fill each muffin cup about three-quarters full with the batter. Bake for 12-15 minutes until golden brown and a toothpick comes out clean. Allow to cool slightly before serving warm, with extra maple syrup for dipping if you like.
