Baked Blueberry Donuts

Adapted from peanutbutterandjilly.com

Prep5 min
Cook25 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup melted regular or vegan butter
  • 1 1/4 cup regular milk, oat milk, or almond milk
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 2 cup regular or gluten free all purpose flour
  • 1 cup fresh or frozen blueberries
  • 1/2 cup powdered sugar
  • 1 tbsp regular milk, oat milk, or almond milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Instructions

  1. Start by preheating your oven to 350°F.
  2. In a big mixing bowl, combine vegan butter, almond or oat milk, baking soda, baking powder, sugar, and vanilla.
  3. Gently mix in the flour until just combined.
  4. Carefully fold in the blueberries into the batter.
  5. Pour the batter into 14-16 greased donut molds, being cautious not to overfill.
  6. Bake for around 20 minutes.
  7. Feel free to add a glaze, but they’re delightful as is!
