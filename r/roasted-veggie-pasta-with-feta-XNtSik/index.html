Roasted Veggie Pasta with Feta

Adapted from thishealthytable.com

Prep5 min
Cook15 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 1 pound fusilli (or other chunky pasta)
  • 6 ounce block of feta
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 1 red onion, cut into thin wedges
  • 2 small zucchini (or 1 large), diced into 1/2 in pieces
  • 1 orange bell pepper, diced into 1/2 in pieces
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 teaspoons kosher salt
  • 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons of freshly squeezed lemon juice
  • 2 cups fresh baby arugula

Instructions

  1. Heat your oven to 400 degrees F.
  2. On a parchment-lined baking sheet, add the feta and veggies. Drizzle with 1 tablespoon olive oil and sprinkle 1 teaspoon salt over everything.
  3. Bake for 15 minutes, until the tomatoes burst.
  4. Cook the pasta in well-salted water following the package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  5. Once the veggies and feta are ready, mix them with the pasta in a large bowl. Stir until the feta is evenly mixed in. Add the remaining olive oil, salt, pepper, lemon juice, and arugula, and gently combine.
  6. Enjoy right away or store in the fridge for up to 4 days.
