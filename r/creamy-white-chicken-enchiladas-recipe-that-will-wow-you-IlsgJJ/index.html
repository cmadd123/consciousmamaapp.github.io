Creamy White Chicken Enchiladas Recipe That Will Wow You

Adapted from nayaplates.com

Prep15 min
Cook30 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 2 cups Shredded Chicken (Rotisserie chicken saves prep time.)
  • 8 oz Softened Cream Cheese (Neufchâtel is a lighter alternative.)
  • 1 tsp Garlic Powder (Consider fresh minced garlic for more flavor.)
  • 1 cup Shredded Monterey Jack Cheese (Cheddar or mozzarella can be used.)
  • 2 tbsp Butter (Olive oil is a lighter option.)
  • 2 tbsp Flour (Gluten-free flour is a suitable substitute.)
  • 2 cups Chicken Broth (Use vegetable broth for vegetarian option.)
  • 1 cup Sour Cream (Greek yogurt is a lower-fat substitute.)
  • 4 oz Diced Green Chiles (Jalapeños can be added for more heat.)
  • 1 tbsp Taco Seasoning (Homemade blend adds personal touch.)
  • 8 tortillas Flour Tortillas (Corn tortillas are gluten-free.)

Instructions

  1. Start by preheating your oven to 350°F (175°C) and greasing a 9x13-inch baking dish.
  2. In a medium bowl, mix together the shredded chicken, softened cream cheese, garlic powder, and half of the shredded cheese until well combined. Stuff this mixture into tortillas, rolling them up and placing seam-side down in the dish.
  3. In a saucepan over medium heat, melt the butter. Add in the flour and taco seasoning to make a roux, cooking for 1-2 minutes.
  4. Slowly whisk in the chicken broth until the mixture is smooth and thick. Stir in 1/2 cup of shredded cheese, then fold in the sour cream and diced green chiles.
  5. Pour the sauce over the rolled tortillas and top with the remaining cheese.
  6. Bake uncovered for 22-25 minutes, then broil for 2-3 minutes until the cheese is golden brown.
