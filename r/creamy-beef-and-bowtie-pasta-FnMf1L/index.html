Creamy Beef and Bowtie Pasta

Adapted from tastyease.com

Prep15 min
Cook30 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 8 oz bowtie pasta
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 cups beef broth
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • Salt to taste
  • Pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley, chopped, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Boil the bowtie pasta as directed on the package, then drain and set it aside.
  2. In a big skillet, cook the ground beef over medium heat until it's browned and cooked through.
  3. Add the chopped onions and minced garlic to the skillet, cooking until the onions are soft and clear.
  4. Stir in the beef broth and heavy cream, letting it come to a gentle simmer.
  5. Mix in the cooked pasta and shredded cheese, stirring until everything is nice and creamy.
  6. Add salt and pepper to taste for extra flavor.
  7. Top with fresh parsley before serving for a lovely touch.
