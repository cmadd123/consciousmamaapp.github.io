Irresistible Garlic Parmesan Crockpot Chicken in 6 Hours

Adapted from recipesbestof.com

Prep10 min
Cook360 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 1.5 lbs baby potatoes, halved
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley

Instructions

  1. Add chicken breasts and potatoes to the crockpot.
  2. Combine parmesan, garlic, oregano, basil, salt, pepper, and olive oil in a bowl.
  3. Drizzle the mixture over the chicken and potatoes.
  4. Set the crockpot to low for 6 hours or high for 3 hours.
  5. Garnish with fresh parsley before serving.
