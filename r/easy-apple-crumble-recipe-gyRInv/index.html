Easy Apple Crumble Recipe

Adapted from goldfoodie.com

Prep15 min
Cook35 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds Granny Smith apples (peeled and cubed)
  • 1 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 0.5 cup white sugar
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 0.5 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup brown sugar (loosely packed)
  • 0.5 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 0.5 cup unsalted butter (melted)
  • 1 pinch salt
  • vanilla ice cream (optional)

Instructions

  1. Set your oven to 350°F (180°C) to preheat.
  2. Peel the apples and cut them into 1/2-inch cubes.
  3. In a big bowl, mix the apples with flour, sugar, lemon juice, and cinnamon until they’re well coated. Pour into a 1.5-quart baking dish.
  4. In another bowl, combine oats, flour, brown sugar, baking powder, cinnamon, melted butter, and salt until crumbly.
  5. Evenly sprinkle the crumbly mixture over the apples. Don’t press it down.
  6. Put the baking dish on a sheet tray and bake for 30–40 minutes, until the topping is golden and the filling is bubbling.
  7. Allow it to cool for 10–15 minutes before serving.
  8. Enjoy warm, and add vanilla ice cream if you like.
