One Hour Dinner Rolls

Adapted from kleinworthco.com

Prep45 min
Cook15 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups warm water (95-105 degrees)
  • 1 tablespoon yeast (I used rapid yeast)
  • 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
  • 2 tablespoons butter (softened)
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 4 cups all-purpose flour (more or less as needed (I used 3-3/4))
  • 3 tablespoons butter (melted )
  • sea salt flakes (for sprinkling on top)

Instructions

  1. In your mixer bowl, mix the yeast and sugar into warm water. Let it sit for five minutes until it gets foamy. (It might take longer if it's chilly.)
  2. Stir in the butter, salt, and 3 cups of flour until everything is blended.
  3. Gradually add more flour, ¼ cup at a time, until the dough pulls away from the bowl and feels a bit sticky. (Don't overdo the flour!)
  4. Knead the dough with your mixer for 3-5 minutes until it's smooth.
  5. Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and let it rise in a warm spot for 20 minutes.
  6. Set your oven to 400 degrees and spray a 9 x 13 casserole dish with cooking spray.
  7. Gently punch down the dough and cut it into 20 equal pieces.
  8. Shape each piece into a ball and arrange them evenly in the prepared dish.
  9. Brush the tops with melted butter, sprinkle with sea salt, and let them rise for another 15-20 minutes.
  10. Bake for 13-15 minutes until they're golden brown. (You can broil for 1-2 minutes for an extra golden top.)
  11. Take them out and brush with more butter.
  12. Allow to cool slightly before serving.
