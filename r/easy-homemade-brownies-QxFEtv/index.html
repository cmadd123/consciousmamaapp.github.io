Easy Homemade Brownies

Adapted from mytxkitchen.com

Prep5 min
Cook30 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • ½ cup unsalted Butter, (melted and cooled)
  • 1 cup Granulated Sugar
  • 1 teaspoon Vanilla Extract
  • 2 large Eggs
  • ¼ teaspoon Baking Soda
  • ¼ teaspoon Salt
  • ½ cup All-Purpose Flour
  • ½ cup Cocoa Powder
  • 1 ½ cups Chocolate Chips*

Instructions

  1. Start by preheating your oven to 350°F. Line a 9”X9” metal baking pan with parchment or foil. Melt the butter and let it cool.
  2. In a large bowl, mix the cooled butter, granulated sugar, and vanilla extract. Add the eggs and stir until everything is well blended.
  3. Combine the dry ingredients—baking soda, salt, flour, and cocoa powder—with the wet mixture. Mix until just combined, then fold in the chocolate chips. The batter will be thick.
  4. Pour the batter into your prepared pan and bake at 350°F for 25-30 minutes. Allow to cool for 30 minutes before slicing.
