Classic Shrimp Boil

Adapted from homeasyrecipes.com

Prep15 min
Cook20 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 1.5 pounds fresh shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • Optional: 1 pound smoked sausage, sliced into thick rounds
  • 4 ears corn on the cob, cut into smaller chunks
  • 1.5 pounds baby potatoes, washed
  • Optional: mushrooms, cherry tomatoes, or bell peppers for variation
  • 3 tablespoons Old Bay seasoning
  • 4 garlic cloves, smashed
  • 2 bay leaves
  • Lemon wedges, for serving

Instructions

  1. Give the baby potatoes a good wash and cut the corn cobs into smaller pieces. If you have smoked sausage, slice it into thick rounds. Peel and devein the shrimp if needed.
  2. In a large pot, bring water to a rolling boil. Stir in Old Bay seasoning, smashed garlic cloves, and bay leaves for flavor. Add the baby potatoes and cook for about 10 minutes until they soften.
  3. Next, toss in the corn chunks and smoked sausage (if using). Keep boiling for another 5 minutes to cook everything through.
  4. Now, add the peeled and deveined shrimp to the boiling pot. Cook for just 2 to 3 minutes until the shrimp turn pink and opaque. Don’t overcook to keep them tender.
  5. Drain everything in a colander and transfer to a large serving platter or spread it out on a newspaper-lined table for fun. Top with lemon wedges and sprinkle more Old Bay if you like.
