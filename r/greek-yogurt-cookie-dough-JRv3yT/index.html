Greek Yogurt Cookie Dough

Adapted from cookedbymomy.com

Prep5 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 180 grams Greek yogurt
  • 15 grams whey protein powder (vanilla, cookie dough, or unflavored)
  • 20 grams peanut butter (creamy)
  • Sweetener of choice, to taste
  • 1 teaspoon chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. In a mixing bowl, blend together Greek yogurt, protein powder, peanut butter, and your preferred sweetener until it’s nice and creamy.
  2. If the mixture is too thick, slowly add milk until you reach your desired consistency.
  3. Gently fold in the chocolate chips to finish your cookie dough.
  4. You can enjoy it right away or pop it in the fridge for a refreshing treat later.
