Creamy Crockpot Marry Me Chicken

Adapted from flavorfullrecipes.com

Prep10 min
Cook180 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (about 1.5 lbs)
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese
  • 1/2 cup sun-dried tomatoes, chopped (packed in oil preferred)
  • 3-4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)
  • Salt &amp; black pepper, to taste
  • 2 tablespoons fresh basil, chopped (for garnish)
  • Extra parmesan for serving

Instructions

  1. Dry the chicken breasts with paper towels and season both sides with salt and pepper.
  2. Lay the chicken breasts at the bottom of the Crockpot.
  3. Top the chicken with chopped sun-dried tomatoes and minced garlic.
  4. Add heavy cream and chicken broth over the mixture.
  5. Sprinkle Italian seasoning and red pepper flakes (if desired) on top.
  6. Finish by adding grated parmesan cheese.
  7. Cover and cook on LOW for 3–4 hours or HIGH for 2–2.5 hours, until the chicken is tender and juices run clear.
  8. If the sauce seems thin, take out the chicken and whisk in 1 tablespoon of cornstarch mixed with 2 tablespoons of cold water. Return the chicken and cook for 15 more minutes. For a thick sauce, add a splash of broth.
  9. Mix in chopped fresh basil. Taste and adjust the seasoning if needed.
  10. Serve the chicken in shallow bowls or on a platter, drizzling extra sauce on top and garnishing with more parmesan and basil.
