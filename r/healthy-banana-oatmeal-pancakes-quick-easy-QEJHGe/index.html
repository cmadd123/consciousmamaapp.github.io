Healthy Banana Oatmeal Pancakes (Quick & Easy!)

Adapted from thelazydish.com

Serves4

Ingredients

  • 2 ripe bananas
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup oats
  • Mix-ins and toppings of your choice (vanilla, cinnamon, berries, nuts, etc.)

Instructions

  1. Heat your skillet on low.
  2. Blend bananas, eggs, and oats on low speed until mixed.
  3. Spray or grease the skillet, then pour about 1/6 of the batter for each pancake. Smaller pancakes work best.
  4. Cook until the first side is set, then flip. If adding walnuts or blueberries, gently press them into the uncooked side before flipping.
  5. Flip the pancakes and cook until done; repeat with the remaining batter.
  6. Enjoy with syrup, butter, peanut butter, or your favorite toppings.
