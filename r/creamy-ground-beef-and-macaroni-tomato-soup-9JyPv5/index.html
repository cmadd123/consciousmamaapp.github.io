Creamy Ground Beef and Macaroni Tomato Soup

Adapted from sixsistersstuff.com

Prep5 min
Cook30 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 1 pound lean ground beef
  • 1 onion (diced)
  • 3 teaspoons minced garlic
  • 2 teaspoons salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon oregano
  • 4 cups chicken stock
  • 26 ounces spaghetti sauce
  • 14 ounces diced tomatoes (drained)
  • 4 ounces cream cheese (room temperature)
  • ¼ cup fresh basil (roughly chopped, divided)
  • 1 ½ cups whole wheat elbow macaroni (or regular elbow macaroni)

Instructions

  1. In a large skillet over medium heat, brown the ground beef with the onion until the beef is cooked through. Add the garlic and sauté for another minute.
  2. Season the beef with salt, pepper, and oregano, stirring everything together well.
  3. Add the chicken stock, spaghetti sauce, and diced tomatoes. Bring the mixture to a simmer and let it cook for 10 minutes.
  4. Stir in the cream cheese until it's fully blended, then mix in half of the fresh basil.
  5. Add the pasta, give it a good stir, and cover. Cook for another 10 minutes or until the pasta is tender.
  6. Serve in bowls and top with the remaining basil.
