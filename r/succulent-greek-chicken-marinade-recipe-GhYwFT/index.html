Succulent Greek Chicken Marinade Recipe

Adapted from tiffanirecipes.com

Prep10 min
Cook30 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 3-4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 4 cloves fresh garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup freshly squeezed lemon juice
  • 1/3 cup extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • Fresh parsley (optional, for garnish)

Instructions

  1. In a big bowl, mix together olive oil, lemon juice, minced garlic, oregano, cumin, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper until everything is blended well.
  2. Place the chicken breasts in the marinade and make sure they’re nicely coated all over.
  3. Cover the bowl or move it to a resealable bag; let it marinate in the fridge for at least 30 minutes or up to 4 hours.
  4. Heat your grill to medium-high (400°F) or preheat the oven to 375°F.
  5. Cook the chicken on the grill for about 6-7 minutes on each side, or bake on a greased tray for 25-30 minutes until golden brown and fully cooked (165°F inside).
  6. Allow the chicken to rest for five minutes before serving.
