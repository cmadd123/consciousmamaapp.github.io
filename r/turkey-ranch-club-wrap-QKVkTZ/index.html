Turkey Ranch Club Wrap

Adapted from tartamour.com

Prep10 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 1 large flour tortilla or wrap
  • 3 ounces sliced turkey breast
  • 2 slices of cooked bacon
  • 1/4 cup shredded lettuce
  • 2 slices of tomato
  • 1/4 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 2 tablespoons ranch dressing
  • 1 tablespoon mayonnaise (optional)
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Start by placing the large flour tortilla on a clean surface like a cutting board or plate.
  2. Spread ranch dressing all over the tortilla. For a creamier taste, mix in some mayonnaise before spreading.
  3. In the middle of the tortilla, lay down the sliced turkey breast in a straight line.
  4. Add the cooked bacon slices on top of the turkey. Crumbling the bacon can help with rolling if you prefer.
  5. Evenly sprinkle shredded lettuce over the turkey and bacon.
  6. Place tomato slices on the lettuce, spreading them out nicely.
  7. Top everything with shredded cheddar cheese and season with salt and pepper to taste.
  8. Gently fold the sides of the tortilla over the filling, then roll it tightly from the bottom up, keeping it compact.
  9. Slice the wrap in half for easier serving.
  10. Enjoy right away, or wrap it in plastic or foil for later.
