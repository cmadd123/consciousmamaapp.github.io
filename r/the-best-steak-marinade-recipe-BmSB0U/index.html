The Best Steak Marinade Recipe

Adapted from cookingfrog.com

Prep10 min
Cook12 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • ⅓ cup of lemon juice
  • ½ cup of olive oil
  • ¼ cup of Worcestershire sauce
  • ⅓ cup of soy sauce
  • Tablespoon of garlic
  • minced
  • 2 Tablespoons of Italian seasoning
  • Teaspoon of pepper
  • ½ teaspoon of salt
  • red pepper (pinch)

Instructions

  1. In a small bowl, mix together olive oil, soy sauce, Worcestershire sauce, lemon juice, Italian seasoning, minced garlic, salt, pepper, and a pinch of red pepper.
  2. Place the steaks in a large bowl and pour the marinade over them. Let them soak overnight or at least for two hours.
  3. Heat your skillet on high and add the steaks. Cook for 3 minutes, then flip them and cook for another 3 minutes.
  4. Lower the heat and continue cooking the steaks to your liking. I usually go for about 10 minutes for medium well.
  5. Finish by adding butter on top of the steaks and garnish with fresh parsley.
