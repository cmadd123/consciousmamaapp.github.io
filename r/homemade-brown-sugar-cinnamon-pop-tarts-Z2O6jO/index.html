Homemade Brown Sugar Cinnamon Pop Tarts

Adapted from everydaypie.com

Prep25 min
Cook35 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 2–½ cups (300 grams) all-purpose flour, more for work surface
  • 1 tablespoon powdered sugar
  • ½ teaspoon (2 grams) coarse kosher salt (see note)
  • 16 tablespoons (230 grams) unsalted butter, cold, cubed
  • ⅓ cup (75 grams) whole milk
  • ½ cup (100 grams) packed dark brown sugar
  • 3 tablespoons (40 grams) unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 tablespoon (16 grams) all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • Egg wash
  • 1 cup (115 grams) powdered sugar
  • 2 tablespoons milk, more as needed
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. In a food processor, combine flour, powdered sugar, and salt. Pulse to mix, then add butter and pulse 7 times. With the processor running, add milk until a ball forms. Wrap the dough in plastic wrap, shape into a rectangle, and roll it out. Chill for at least 2 hours or overnight.
  2. In a small bowl, mix brown sugar, melted butter, flour, and cinnamon until well blended.
  3. Take the chilled dough out and let it sit for 5 to 10 minutes. Divide it in half. On a floured surface, roll one piece into a 9-by-13 rectangle, about ⅛-inch thick. Cut out as many 3-by-4-inch rectangles as possible. Repeat with the other half. Gather scraps, roll out, and cut more rectangles. You should have 16 to 18 total. Line a baking sheet with parchment and place half the rectangles on it. Brush egg wash around the edges. Add 1 heaping tablespoon of filling to each rectangle, spreading it out while leaving a ½-inch border. Top with remaining rectangles, sealing edges well. Crimp edges with a fork and trim for a neat look. Chill for 30 minutes. Poke holes in the top of each to let steam escape.
  4. Preheat your oven to 375°F and position a rack in the middle. Bake the pop tarts for about 25 minutes until the bottoms are lightly golden. Cool on a wire rack for at least 1 hour. In a small bowl, whisk powdered sugar, milk, cinnamon, and vanilla until thick enough to hold its shape when drizzled. Spoon 1 tablespoon of glaze onto each cooled pop tart and smooth it out. Let the glaze set for 15 minutes before serving.
