Chicken Kabobs

Adapted from simplejoy.com

Prep25 min
Cook15 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 1 pound boneless skinless chicken breasts (cut in bite-sized pieces (453.6 grams))
  • 1 medium zucchini (sliced)
  • 1 red onion (diced large)
  • 1 red bell pepper (diced large)
  • 1 orange bell pepper (diced large)
  • 1/3 cup olive oil ((66.7 grams or 78.9 ml))
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce ((63.8 grams or 59.1 ml))
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • 2 tablespoon brown sugar
  • 2 tablespoons balsamic vinegar
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

Instructions

  1. Cut the chicken into small, bite-sized pieces.
  2. In a container, mix the marinade ingredients. Add the chicken and stir well. Cover and refrigerate for 30 minutes to 24 hours.
  3. Once marinated, heat the grill to medium.
  4. Assemble the kabobs by alternating chicken and vegetables on the skewers. Aim for 6 kabobs, using two veggies and a piece of chicken each time.
  5. Grill the kabobs for 5 to 6 minutes. Flip them using tongs and grill for another 5 to 6 minutes, until the chicken reaches 165°F.
  6. Take the kabobs off the grill. Let them cool for a couple of minutes before serving. Enjoy!
