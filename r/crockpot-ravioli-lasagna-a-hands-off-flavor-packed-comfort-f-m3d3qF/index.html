Crockpot Ravioli Lasagna: A Hands-Off, Flavor-Packed Comfort Food Masterpiece

Adapted from erinrecipes.com

Prep10 min
Cook240 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 can (24 oz) pasta sauce
  • 1 package (25 oz) frozen cheese ravioli
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon Italian seasoning

Instructions

  1. Brown the ground beef in a skillet over medium heat, then drain any excess fat.
  2. Mix in the pasta sauce, garlic powder, and Italian seasoning. Let it simmer for 5 minutes.
  3. In a greased slow cooker, add a thin layer of the meat sauce.
  4. Place half of the frozen ravioli on top of the sauce.
  5. Spread half of the remaining meat sauce and half of each cheese over the ravioli.
  6. Repeat the layers, finishing with a layer of cheese on top.
  7. Cover and cook on low for 4-5 hours until everything is heated through and the cheese is melted.
  8. Serve it hot and enjoy your meal!
