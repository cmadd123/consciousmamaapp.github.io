Homemade Pizza Pockets

Adapted from passionforsavings.com

Prep10 min
Cook13 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 1 refrigerated tube of pizza crust
  • 6 Tablespoons of pasta sauce
  • 1 cup of mozzarella cheese
  • Approximately 12 pepperoni slices
  • Sprinkle of oregano leaves OPTIONAL

Instructions

  1. Start by heating your oven to 425F. Unroll the pizza crust and flatten it on parchment paper or a floured surface.
  2. Slice the pizza crust into 6 squares.
  3. On half of each square, spread about 1 tablespoon of pasta sauce, add mozzarella cheese, and place 2 pepperoni slices.
  4. Fold each square over to create triangles and use a fork to seal the edges.
  5. If you'd like, brush a little oil on top of each triangle and sprinkle with dried oregano.
  6. Bake in the oven at 425F for 10 to 13 minutes until they turn golden brown.
  7. Enjoy with a small bowl of pasta sauce for dipping.
