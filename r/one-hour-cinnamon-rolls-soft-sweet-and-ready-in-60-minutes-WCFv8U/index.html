One Hour Cinnamon Rolls: Soft, Sweet, and Ready in 60 Minutes!

Adapted from munchtwist.com

Prep20 min
Cook20 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 ¼ tsp instant yeast
  • 1 cup warm milk (110°F)
  • ¼ cup granulated sugar
  • 1 egg
  • ¼ cup melted butter
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ⅔ cup brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp ground cinnamon
  • ⅓ cup melted butter
  • 4 oz cream cheese
  • 1 cup powdered sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Heat the milk and stir in the yeast; let it sit until it gets nice and foamy.
  2. Combine flour, sugar, salt, butter, egg, and the milk mixture to form a dough.
  3. Knead the dough, then let it rest for 10 minutes.
  4. Roll out the dough, spread some butter, and sprinkle with cinnamon-sugar.
  5. Roll it up tightly, slice into pieces, and place them in a baking pan.
  6. Allow the rolls to rise for 20 minutes.
  7. Bake in a preheated oven at 375°F for 18-22 minutes.
  8. Prepare the frosting and spread it over the warm rolls.
