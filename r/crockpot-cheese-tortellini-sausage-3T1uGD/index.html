Crockpot Cheese Tortellini Sausage

Adapted from nourishwithrecipes.com

Prep15 min
Cook30 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb Italian sausage (Mild or spicy, your preference)
  • 1 pkg Cheese tortellini (Frozen)
  • 24 oz Jar of marinara sauce
  • 1 cup Chicken broth

Instructions

  1. In a skillet over medium heat, cook the Italian sausage until browned. Drain any extra grease.
  2. Transfer the sausage to a slow cooker. Add the tortellini, marinara sauce, and chicken broth.
  3. Set your slow cooker on low for 6-8 hours or high for 3-4 hours, until the tortellini is tender.
  4. Serve it warm and enjoy your meal!
