Crockpot Cheesy Ranch Beef Pasta Shells

Adapted from juliameals.com

Prep20 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 packet ranch seasoning mix
  • 1 can cream of mushroom soup (10.5 oz)
  • 1 cup beef broth
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1 pound medium pasta shells
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley for garnish (optional)

Instructions

  1. Start by browning the ground beef in a skillet over medium heat. Drain any extra fat. In a bowl, combine ranch seasoning, cream of mushroom soup, and beef broth. Shred the cheeses and cook pasta shells until al dente, then drain and set aside.
  2. In your crockpot, layer half of the cooked ground beef, then half of the pasta shells. Pour half of the ranch mixture over this, and sprinkle half of the shredded cheddar and mozzarella on top. Repeat with the remaining ingredients.
  3. Cover the crockpot and cook on low for 2-3 hours, until the cheese is melted and bubbly. Add salt and pepper to taste.
  4. If you like, garnish with fresh parsley and serve hot.
