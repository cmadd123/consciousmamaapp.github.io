Best Homemade Chocolate Chip Cookies

Adapted from cookiesfordays.com

Prep15 min
Cook10 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup (167g) salted butter, softened
  • 1/2 cup (123g) brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup (115g) granulated sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp vanilla extract
  • 2 cups (304g) all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 cup (200g) favorite chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Set your oven to 350° F.
  2. In a mixing bowl, beat the butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar until it's light and fluffy.
  3. Add the egg and vanilla extract, mixing until everything is blended well.
  4. Stir in the flour, baking soda, and salt until a soft dough forms that isn't sticky.
  5. Gently fold in your favorite chocolate chips.
  6. Scoop out 1 1/2 tbsp portions of dough and place them on your cookie sheet. Use parchment paper, a silicone mat, or leave it ungreased.
  7. Bake for 8-10 minutes at 350° F, until the cookies spread and the edges start to turn golden brown.
  8. Let the cookies cool on the pan for 5 minutes before moving them to a wire rack.
