Buffalo Chicken Dip Classic

Adapted from sweetsbyaria.com

Prep5 min
Cook20 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ pounds cooked chicken breast, shredded (approximately 3 large breasts)
  • 8 ounces cream cheese, softened
  • ⅔ cup ranch dressing
  • ⅔ cup Frank's RedHot Buffalo sauce
  • 1 ½ cups shredded cheddar cheese

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (177°C).
  2. In a big bowl, mix together the shredded chicken, softened cream cheese, ranch dressing, buffalo sauce, and 1 cup of shredded cheddar cheese until smooth.
  3. Pour the chicken mixture into a 1.5-quart baking dish and top it with the remaining ½ cup of shredded cheddar cheese.
  4. Bake in the oven for 20 to 25 minutes, until the cheese is golden and the dip is bubbling at the edges.
  5. Serve hot with chips, carrot sticks, or celery, as you like.
