Fudgy Chewy Browkies (Brookies)

Adapted from savvybites.co.uk

Prep15 min
Cook15 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 95 g plain (all-purpose) flour
  • 2 tablespoons cocoa powder
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 225 g dark chocolate (chopped)
  • 56 g butter (salted or unsalted)
  • 2 eggs *room temperature
  • 100 g caster or granulated sugar
  • 90 g Demerara or raw sugar (*see notes)
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 85 g dark or milk chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Start by preheating your oven to 160ºC (320ºF) with a fan or 170ºC (338ºF) for conventional. Line a baking tray with parchment paper.
  2. In a heatproof bowl, mix chopped dark chocolate and butter. Set the bowl over simmering water, ensuring it doesn't touch the water. Stir until melted and smooth, then add vanilla extract.
  3. In a medium bowl, use an electric whisk to beat together caster sugar, demerara sugar, and eggs on high. Whisk until light, thick, and fluffy, about 5 minutes.
  4. Sift flour, cocoa powder, baking powder, and salt into another bowl, then mix in the chocolate chips to avoid lumps and combine everything evenly.
  5. Gently fold the melted chocolate and butter into the whipped egg and sugar mixture. Be careful to keep it airy.
  6. Using a spatula, carefully fold the dry ingredients into the wet mix until just combined, avoiding deflation of the batter.
  7. Drop tablespoons of batter onto the lined tray, leaving space for spreading. Bake for 12-14 minutes until set and crackly on top.
  8. Once out of the oven, sprinkle with sea salt if you like. Let cool on the tray for about 10 minutes before moving to a cooling rack.
