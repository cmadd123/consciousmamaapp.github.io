Street Corn Chicken Chili Recipe

Adapted from emilysplate.com

Prep15 min
Cook50 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 to 4 jalapeno peppers, seeds removed and diced
  • 6 to 8 ears corn on the cob, kernels cut from cob
  • Kosher salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 6 to 8 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 pounds ground chicken
  • 3 tablespoons chili powder
  • 2 tablespoons paprika
  • 1 tablespoon cumin
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 1 tablespoon chicken base (such as Better Than Bouillon)
  • A few dashes hot sauce of choice
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 1 cup chopped cilantro
  • 3 tablespoons lime juice
  • Optional toppings: more cilantro, crumbled cotija cheese, chili powder, lime wedges

Instructions

  1. In a large soup pot over medium heat, warm the olive oil. Add diced onion, jalapenos, corn, kosher salt, and pepper. Cook for 7 to 9 minutes until veggies soften and onion is translucent.
  2. Add minced garlic and ground chicken. Mix in chili powder, paprika, cumin, and adjust salt and pepper. Cook for another 7 to 9 minutes, breaking up the chicken as it browns.
  3. Pour in chicken broth and stir in chicken base. Raise the heat to bring it to a gentle boil, then lower to medium-low. Let it simmer uncovered for 25 to 30 minutes to thicken and blend flavors.
  4. Mix in hot sauce, sour cream, chopped cilantro, and lime juice. Cook for 5 to 10 minutes, stirring gently until creamy and combined. Taste and adjust seasoning as needed.
  5. Scoop chili into bowls and top with extra cilantro, crumbled cotija cheese, a dash of chili powder, and lime wedges if you prefer. Serve hot and enjoy!
