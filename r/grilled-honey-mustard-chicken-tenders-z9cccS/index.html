Grilled Honey Mustard Chicken Tenders

Adapted from damndelicious.net

Prep135 min
Cook10 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup coarse ground Dijon mustard
  • 1/2 shallot (diced)
  • 3 tablespoons honey
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh rosemary
  • 1 tablespoon freshly squeezed lemon juice
  • Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper (to taste)
  • 1 1/2 pounds chicken tenderloins
  • 1 tablespoon canola oil

Instructions

  1. In a medium bowl, mix Dijon, shallot, honey, olive oil, rosemary, lemon juice, 1 teaspoon salt, and 1/2 teaspoon pepper. Refrigerate half for later.
  2. Place chicken and the Dijon mixture in a gallon Ziploc bag or large bowl. Marinate for 2 hours to overnight, up to 8 hours. Drain chicken from marinade.
  3. Heat the grill to medium.
  4. Coat chicken with canola oil and season with salt and pepper. Grill chicken, turning occasionally, until it reaches 165°F, about 8 minutes.
  5. Brush with the reserved Dijon mixture and cook for another 1-2 minutes.
  6. Enjoy your meal right away!
