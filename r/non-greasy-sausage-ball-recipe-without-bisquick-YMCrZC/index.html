Non Greasy Sausage Ball Recipe Without Bisquick

Adapted from butterandbaggage.com

Prep20 min
Cook15 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 3 teaspoons baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 2 tablespoons butter (cold)
  • 1 lb. breakfast sausage (uncooked)
  • 8 ounces sharp cheddar cheese (grated)

Instructions

  1. Start by preheating your oven to 400º. Line a rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. In a stand mixer or food processor, combine flour, baking powder, salt, and butter until mixed. Alternatively, use your fingers to blend the butter into the flour. Stir in grated cheese, then gradually add sausage, mixing until combined. You can also use your hands for this part.
  3. Scoop out tablespoon-sized portions and roll them into balls. Place them on the prepared baking sheet.
  4. Bake for 15 minutes, or until they’re just slightly browned. Enjoy them warm!
