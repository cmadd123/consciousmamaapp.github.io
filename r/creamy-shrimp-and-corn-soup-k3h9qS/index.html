Creamy Shrimp and Corn Soup

Adapted from diaryofarecipecollector.com

Serves4

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups onions, bell peppers, celery (I used a frozen Seasoned Blend mix)
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 large red potato, peeled and chopped into 1 in. cubes
  • 1 1/2 tsp creole seasoning (divided)
  • 1 lb. medium shrimp (70/90 ct.)
  • 14.5 oz. can whole kernel corn, drained
  • 14.5 oz. can cream-style corn
  • 4 oz. cream cheese, cubed
  • 2 cups half and half
  • Optional: Chopped fresh parsley

Instructions

  1. In a large pot, melt butter and olive oil together.
  2. Add onions, bell peppers, celery, and potato. Sauté for 10 minutes, adding 3/4 tsp of creole seasoning.
  3. Sprinkle the remaining creole seasoning on the shrimp, then mix into the veggies. Cook for 4 to 5 minutes over medium to medium-high heat.
  4. Stir in the corn, cream cheese, and half and half. Bring to a gentle simmer, stirring until the cream cheese melts. Cook on low until smooth.
  5. Garnish with fresh parsley if you like.
  6. This soup pairs nicely with bread and a salad.
