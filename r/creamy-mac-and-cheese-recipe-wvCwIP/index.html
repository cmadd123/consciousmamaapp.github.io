Creamy Mac and Cheese Recipe

Adapted from pumpkinnspice.com

Prep20 min
Cook15 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 1 pound cavatappi shells
  • ¼ cup unsalted butter
  • ¼ cup all-purpose flour ((see note below))
  • ¾ teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 cup milk, any kind ((I used 2%))
  • 1¼ cups heavy whipping cream
  • 1 cup shredded sharp cheddar cheese
  • 1 cup shredded Mozzarella cheese

Instructions

  1. Boil water in a large pot and cook the noodles until al dente according to the package. Drain and set them aside.
  2. In a medium pot, melt the butter. Whisk in flour, salt, and pepper to create a roux. *For extra flavor, consider adding garlic powder, paprika, or onion powder.*
  3. Gradually mix in milk and heavy cream, whisking until everything is blended. Heat over medium until it bubbles and thickens a bit.
  4. Lower the heat and stir in the cheeses until they melt and become smooth. Toss in the pasta, mixing well. If it's too thick, add 1-2 tablespoons of milk.
  5. Allow the mac and cheese to sit for 5 minutes to thicken up.
