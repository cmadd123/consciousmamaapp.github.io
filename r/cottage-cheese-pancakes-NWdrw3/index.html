Cottage Cheese Pancakes

Adapted from momsdish.com

Prep25 min
Cook20 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups cottage cheese
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1/4 cup canola oil (for frying )

Instructions

  1. Start by mixing the ingredients in this order: cheese, eggs, vanilla, sugar, baking powder, and flour. Stir after each addition.
  2. Heat a skillet with canola oil over medium heat. Cook the pancakes until they're golden brown on both sides.
  3. Enjoy them warm with maple syrup or jam, so the cheese stays nice and melty.
