Easy Breakfast Potatoes

Adapted from tablye.com

Prep10 min
Cook30 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds Russet potatoes
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon Kosher salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder
  • ½ teaspoon paprika
  • ½ teaspoon onion powder
  • 1 tablespoon fresh minced parsley

Instructions

  1. Start by preheating your oven to 400°F (200°C) and prepare a large baking sheet with nonstick foil or a silicone mat.
  2. Peel the Russet potatoes and cut them into ½-inch cubes. Pat them dry with paper towels to help them crisp up.
  3. In a big bowl, mix the diced potatoes with olive oil, salt, pepper, garlic powder, paprika, and onion powder. Toss until they're well coated.
  4. Spread the potatoes out in a single layer on the baking sheet and bake for 20-23 minutes until they start to soften.
  5. Give the potatoes a stir and then broil for another 4-5 minutes until they're golden brown and crispy.
  6. Before serving, sprinkle some fresh parsley on top for a nice touch.
