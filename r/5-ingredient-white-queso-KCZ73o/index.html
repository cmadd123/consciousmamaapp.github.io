5-ingredient white queso

Adapted from thebakingfairy.net

Prep5 min
Cook20 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb pepperjack cheese
  • 8 oz full-fat cream cheese
  • 1/2 cup full-fat sour cream
  • 1 10 oz can original Rotel tomatoes &amp; green chilies (drained)
  • 3/4 cup whole milk (if needed)
  • *optional*
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • *NOTE: I have gotten mixed feedback about using low-fat substitutions so use full-fat dairy for best results!*
  • *NOTE 2: For a smoother queso (try using half white American cheese and half pepperjack!*)

Instructions

  1. In a medium pot on LOW heat, mix the cheese, cream cheese, sour cream, and Rotel together.
  2. Stir often as it heats, allowing the cheeses to melt into a smooth dip. This takes about 15-20 minutes. Add spices or milk if you like.
  3. Serve right away, or keep it warm in a slow cooker set to 'warm.' Enjoy your dip!
