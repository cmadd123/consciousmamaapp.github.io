Healthy Ground Turkey Sloppy Joes (Manwich Copycat)

Adapted from yummymacrolife.com

Prep5 min
Cook15 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb. Lean Ground Turkey (Or Ground Beef)
  • 2 Cloves of Garlic (Fresh or Minced)
  • 8 oz. Tomato Sauce
  • 1/2 cup Ketchup (No sugar option preferred for lower sugar content)
  • 2-4 tbsp. Brown Sugar (Light or Dark Brown Sugar)
  • 2 tbsp. Worcestershire Sauce
  • 1 tsp. Prepared Mustard
  • 1/2 tsp. Garlic Powder
  • 1/2 tsp. Onion Powder
  • Black Pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. In a skillet over medium-high heat, start cooking the ground turkey. Add the garlic once it begins to brown.
  2. While the turkey cooks, combine the other ingredients for the sloppy joe sauce in a small bowl.
  3. After the turkey is fully cooked, drain any extra fat from the skillet.
  4. Pour the homemade sloppy joe sauce into the skillet and reduce the heat to a simmer.
  5. Let it simmer for at least 10 minutes to blend the flavors.
  6. Spoon the mixture onto buns and dig in!
