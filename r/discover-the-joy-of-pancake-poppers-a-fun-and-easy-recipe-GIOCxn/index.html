Discover the Joy of Pancake Poppers: A Fun and Easy Recipe!

Adapted from ladyofrecipes.com

Prep10 min
Cook12 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoon granulated sugar
  • 1 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 3/4 cup milk or buttermilk
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 tablespoon melted butter
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Start by preheating your oven to 375°F. Give a mini muffin tin a good spray with cooking spray.
  2. In a medium bowl, mix the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt until everything is blended well.
  3. In another bowl, combine the milk, egg, melted butter, and vanilla extract. Whisk until it's nice and smooth.
  4. Gently mix the wet ingredients into the dry ones using a wooden spoon. If you have any mix-ins, fold them in now.
  5. Scoop the batter into each mini muffin cup, filling them about two-thirds full. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes until they’re golden and springy.
