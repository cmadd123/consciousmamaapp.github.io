The Best Balsamic Chicken Recipe

Adapted from cookedbysarah.com

Prep15 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • ⅓ cup balsamic vinegar
  • ¼ cup extra virgin olive oil
  • ¼ cup low sodium chicken broth (or stock)
  • 2 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • 1 tablespoon fresh rosemary (minced (or 1 teaspoon dried))
  • ½ teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • 2 pounds boneless skinless chicken thighs (*see note)
  • parsley (chopped)

Instructions

  1. In a large gallon-sized ziplock bag, mix balsamic vinegar, olive oil, chicken broth, minced garlic, honey, rosemary, and Italian seasoning.
  2. Add the chicken to the bag and massage it gently to coat. Refrigerate for 2 hours to overnight. (Placing the bag in a bowl helps catch any spills.)
  3. Take the chicken out of the fridge when you start your grill. Preheat it to medium-high heat for 10 minutes, aiming for about 400°F. Oil the grates, then grill the chicken, smooth side down, for 7-8 minutes. Flip and grill for another 5-7 minutes until it reaches 165°F. Let it rest under foil for 10 minutes before garnishing with parsley.
  4. Remove the chicken from the fridge when preheating your oven to 400°F. Place the chicken in a baking dish or on a baking sheet and bake for 15 to 20 minutes, until it hits 165°F.
