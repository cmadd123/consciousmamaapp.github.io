Garlic Grilled Shrimp Skewers

Adapted from downshiftology.com

Prep5 min
Cook5 min
Serves4

Ingredients

  • 1 pound large shrimp
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • ¼ cup fresh cilantro (finely chopped)
  • ¼ cup fresh parsley (finely chopped)
  • 4 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • pinch cayenne pepper (adjust to spice preference)

Instructions

  1. In a small bowl, combine olive oil, lemon juice, herbs, garlic, and spices. Whisk until blended.
  2. Put the shrimp in a bowl and pour on ¾ of the marinade. Gently mix to coat, cover, and let marinate for 30 minutes to an hour.
  3. Skewer the shrimp, making sure to grab all the tasty garlic and herbs from the marinade bowl.
  4. Preheat your grill or grill pan to medium-high. Cook the shrimp skewers for 2 to 3 minutes on each side, until they’re pink and opaque.
  5. Transfer the shrimp to a plate and drizzle the remaining marinade on top before serving.
