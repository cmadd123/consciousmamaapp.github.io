Questions? We've got answers.

Is MomRise free? +
Yes! MomRise is free to download and use. All core features are included at no cost.
Can my husband use it too? +
Each family member can have their own profile, and you can assign tasks and events to Mom, Dad, or anyone else. Everyone stays on the same page.
How do the AI learning paths work? +
Tell MomRise about a parenting challenge you're facing, like potty training or sleep struggles. Our AI creates a personalized day-by-day plan with activities, tips, and guidance tailored to your child's age. Each learning path includes suggestions for when things don't go as planned.
How is this different from a regular calendar app? +
Regular calendars show events. MomRise is built specifically for family life: meal planning, child milestone tracking, age-appropriate activities, AI learning paths, and task assignment to family members. It's built for how families actually work.
Is my family's data private? +
Your privacy is our priority. We never sell your personal information or your children's data. All data is stored securely, and you can delete your account and all associated data at any time. Read our full privacy policy for details.
Can I add multiple children? +
Add as many children as you have. Each child gets their own profile with milestones and activities personalized to their age.
