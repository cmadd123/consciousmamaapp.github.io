Questions? We've got answers.

Is Conscious Mama free? +
Yes! Conscious Mama is free to download and use. We believe every busy parent deserves access to helpful tools without financial barriers.
Can my partner use it too? +
Each family member can have their own profile, and you can assign tasks and events to Mom, Dad, or anyone else. Everyone stays on the same page.
How is this different from a regular calendar app? +
Regular calendars show events, but Conscious Mama is designed specifically for family life. Meal planning, child profiles with color coding, task assignment to family members - it's built for how families actually work.
Is my data private and secure? +
Your privacy is our priority. We never sell your personal information. All data is stored securely, and you can delete your account and all associated data at any time.
Can I add multiple children? +
Absolutely! Add as many children as you have. Each child gets their own color-coded profile so you can see at a glance who has what going on.
