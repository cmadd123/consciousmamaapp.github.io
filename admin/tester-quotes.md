# MomRise Tester Quotes

Real testimonial language from early testers. Useful for App Store listing copy, marketing site, creator playbook social proof, and creator-program pitch decks.

Each quote is verbatim from the user. Don't paraphrase. If you need to shorten for display, do so with care and keep "..." between dropped sections.

---

## 2026-06-01 — Early tester (app launch week)

> I absolutely love this app!! What a brilliant idea! "Warm" is the perfect word for it! And my heart just sang when I saw "add picture from a cookbook". You really thought of everything! I looove the names on the calendar. Just brilliant.

**Highlights from this quote:**
- "Warm" — confirms the brand-positioning word we've been using lands authentically with users
- "Add picture from a cookbook" — call-out for the cookbook image-import feature. Worth amplifying in App Store screenshots and creator playbook
- "Names on the calendar" — refers to the per-family-member color-coded names (Mom, Virginia, Sophia, Griffin, Addie etc. on event cards). Already a UX win, worth keeping
- "Thought of everything" — high-praise framing for marketing
- Three exclamation points + "heart just sang" — emotional intensity. This is the kind of language we want to surface when pitching the creator program ("share what you love")
