Bootstrap Week 1 · ~5 hrs

Foundations first. After this week you switch to the weekly rhythm.

Day 1 — Accounts 30 min

  • MomRise IG: confirm it's Professional / Creator mode (Settings → Account → Switch to Professional → "Creator"). Required for Collab posts + Insights.
  • MomRise Pinterest: set up at business.pinterest.com. When you click "Claim your website," DM Collin the verification token — he commits it to momrise.app, you click verify. ~5 min round-trip.
  • Firebase Console: bookmark console.firebase.google.com → MomRise → Storage tab. That's where you'll upload videos.
  • Canva (free tier). Bookmark.
  • CapCut on your phone (free). This is the video editor — better than Reels' built-in.

Day 2 — Pinterest boards 45 min

Create these 10 boards. Names are deliberate — these phrases come from Pinterest's autocomplete and they rank where generic names don't.

  • Easy Toddler Dinners
  • Sheet Pan Family Meals
  • Lunchbox Ideas for Picky Eaters
  • 30-Minute Mom Recipes
  • Make-Ahead Freezer Meals
  • Healthy Meal Prep for Busy Moms
  • Kid-Friendly Snacks Without Sugar
  • Weeknight Dinner Ideas for Families
  • One-Pan Recipes for Tired Moms
  • Family Breakfast Ideas

Don't pin anything yet. Just create the boards with a 1-sentence description each — Pinterest weights board descriptions for SEO. Style: "Quick, kid-approved dinners I actually make on weeknights." Avoid keyword stuffing.

Day 3 — Storage folder 5 min

Firebase Console → Storage → creator_library/ folder already exists (publicly readable, admin-write only).

When you upload a video, just name it descriptively — no subfolders needed:

share-tiktok-to-momrise.mp4 recipe-to-grocery-list.mp4 hands-chopping-onion.mp4

After upload: click the file → copy the access URL (starts with https://firebasestorage.googleapis.com/...) → paste into creator/library/manifest.js with a title and duration. Push the repo. The Creator Library at momrise.app/creator/library/ picks it up on next load.

Day 4 — Five app screen recordings 30 min

The easiest day. Open MomRise, hit record-screen, do each of these once slowly. Trim in CapCut. No audio — creators will voice-over.

  • share-tiktok-to-momrise.mp4 — 7-10s. Open TikTok, tap share, choose MomRise, recipe lands.
  • recipe-to-grocery-list.mp4 — 8-10s. Open a recipe, "add to grocery list," watch it appear.
  • grocery-to-instacart.mp4 — 6-8s. Grocery list → "Send to Instacart" → Instacart opens with items.
  • meal-plan-week.mp4 — 5-7s. Scroll through one week of the meal plan view.
  • family-setup.mp4 — 8-10s. Quick demo of adding a child or family member.

Upload each to Firebase Storage as you finish, add to creator/library/manifest.js.

Day 5 — Cook With Me Reel 90 min

Film one full recipe Reel. This becomes the first MomRise IG post AND the first "example" file creators can reference.

Setup (10 min): pick a recipe from an existing creator's TikTok, import it via the share extension. Phone on a tripod or stack of books, eye-level. Daylight if possible.

Film (45 min for a ~25s Reel):

  • 1-2s hook: "I import all my recipes from TikTok now"
  • Cut to the share-from-TikTok screen recording you already shot
  • You cooking the recipe: 4-5 short cuts
  • 2-3s outro: "Recipe imported from @[creator]. Code [CODE] in bio."

Edit (30 min in CapCut):

  • Add on-screen overlay Paid partnership · #ad in the top-right, visible the whole time
  • Auto-captions, proofread
  • Target 20-30 seconds — sweet spot for TikTok, still works for IG. One master cross-posts to both.

Post (5 min): caption goes #ad Affiliate of @momrise — import recipes from TikTok in one tap. Recipe by @[creator]. Code [CODE] in bio.

Save the final mp4 to Firebase Storage and add to manifest under "Examples" so creators can watch it as a format reference.

Day 6 — One bootstrap pin 30 min

A placeholder until the recipe pin generator workflow kicks in. Pull a still frame from Day 5's Reel, make a 1000×1500 Canva design with recipe name big at top + small MomRise mark, save as pin-template-001.png. Pin to 2 of your boards with a 1-sentence description.

Day 7 — Review 20 min

  • Scroll MomRise IG: does the one post look right?
  • Check Pinterest: pin is live? Don't expect impressions yet — week 1 is flat.
  • TikTok (after Business Account setup): is the first post live? Watch-through %?
  • Add today's date to a tracking sheet (Drive): IG followers + post views, Pinterest impressions + saves, TikTok followers + watch-through, App Store Connect campaign attributions (ct=pinterest-* and ct=tiktok-*). Update weekly.

Weekly rhythm ~3-4 hrs/wk

Front-load on one day if that's easier than spreading.

One filming + editing session 2 hrs

Pick a recipe. Film a Cook With Me Reel using the Day 5 setup. One Reel master cross-posts to BOTH MomRise IG and MomRise TikTok — see TikTok playbook for the upload workflow.

Pin batch ~30 min/week

Once Collin's recipe page generator is live (it is), the pin generator at momrise.app/admin/pin-generator.html drives this. Pin every recipe Collin flags as is_curated once, to its best-matching board.

Volume is not the goal. A correctly-pinned 30 unique recipes beats 200 recycled pins.

Monthly re-pinning: look at which pins earned saves/clicks. Winners get re-pinned to 1-2 adjacent boards with a new image angle. Kill losers — don't try to revive with re-pins.

One IG post per week already filmed

Aim for Tuesday or Thursday evening — these tend to be highest-engagement for parenting content. Not a rule, just a starting hypothesis.

Alternate-week variation 1 hr

Every other week, instead of a Cook With Me Reel, post one of:

  • "What I Feed My Toddler This Week" carousel (7 meal photos + final slide is MomRise weekly plan screenshot)
  • "Pulling a recipe from TikTok into MomRise" 30-second demo Reel
  • Behind-the-scenes "how I pick recipes for the week"

Asset library refresh 15 min, end of week

Save the week's finished Reel master to Firebase Storage as an updated "example finished post" reference for creators. Update caption snippets in the library manifest if a hook worked unusually well. No more hands-only B-roll cuts — creators were unlikely to use someone else's kitchen footage anyway.

TikTok playbook Same filming, second platform

Adds TikTok without doubling production. One filming session → cross-posts to IG + TikTok.

One-time setup: Verified Business Account 15 min

Personal/Creator accounts CAN'T put app-store links in bio (TikTok blocks them with "Action cannot be completed"). Business Account is the only sanctioned path.

  1. TikTok app → Settings → Manage Account → "Switch to Business Account"
  2. Category: Food & Beverage
  3. Bio website URL: apps.apple.com/app/id6758357382?ct=tiktok-brand&mt=8

For per-creator codes, same pattern with ct=tiktok-{CODE} — DM Collin to provision these. App Store Connect Analytics → Acquisition shows installs attributed by campaign (24-hour window).

Never put momrise.app in the TikTok bio link. Pinterest blocked the domain as spam (it's under 6 months old). TikTok may have a similar classifier — the apps.apple.com URL sidesteps the question.

Cadence 3-4 posts/week

Buffer's 11.4M-post regression (Jan 2026): median views per post DROP above 5/week. More posts = more viral-lottery shots, not better typical performance.

The "post daily or the algorithm kills you" thing is a myth. No documented frequency penalty. 3-4/week sustainable beats 7/week burnout.

Style: natural beats polished good news

TikTok's 2026 Next Trend Report (their own primary doc) explicitly says "the era of polished perfection is fading" — audiences want unfiltered, BTS, real-process content.

Implication for filming: phone-shot, natural lighting, face-on-camera, your voice. NOT studio-lit B-roll with music beds + voiceover. "Authentic" doesn't mean low-effort — hook strength, audio clarity, and lighting still matter; the aesthetic just needs to read spontaneous.

The cross-posting workflow save 10+ min/post

Export ONE master from CapCut. Upload to BOTH platforms.

  • No watermarks on the master. If you save the IG version FROM IG to your camera roll, the IG logo is baked into that file — gets downranked on TikTok. Always upload from the original CapCut master, never from a downloaded copy.
  • Audio: see the dedicated audio rules below.

Audio rules applies to all platforms

Voiceover content (your Cook With Me Reels): audio IS your voice — bakes into the master. No swap needed. Upload the same file to TikTok + IG.

Music-bed content (no voice / B-roll / lifestyle cuts): export the master MUTED. At upload time:

  • TikTok: browse the Commercial Music Library, pick a trending sound
  • IG: same idea in IG's native audio library

Two different sounds, one master file. Costs ~30 sec extra per platform.

Why this matters:

  • Discoverability: platform-native audio reportedly lifts TikTok views 48-68% and IG reach ~33% vs imported audio. Same sound across videos = a discovery graph your post enters.
  • Licensing: TikTok's July 2025 Music ToS update requires Business Accounts to use the in-app library — CML license is TikTok-only. Baking it into a master cross-posted to IG creates licensing exposure.
  • Cross-platform detection: TikTok's audio fingerprinting (ACRCloud) is robust to pitch/speed shifts, so file-level tricks don't reliably evade detection.

What NOT to do:

  • Drag music files (Epidemic Sound, YouTube licensed audio) into CapCut as a master audio track
  • Trust "evasion" tricks like a 1% pitch shift or 4px crop — they don't work

First 4 TikTok posts test set

  1. Cook With Me — POV cooking, your standard format. Same Reel as IG. Your voiceover, your face, your script.
  2. "I import all my recipes from TikTok now" — Hands-on-phone demo. Show the share-from-TikTok flow. 15s. Hook in the first 1-2 seconds.
  3. "How I plan a week of dinners in 8 minutes" — Real-time screen recording of you using MomRise, with light voiceover. Slow pacing is fine — the value is showing it really takes 8 min.
  4. Stitch a viral mom-food TikTok — Find a "what do I make for dinner?" post in your FYP. Stitch with "Here's what I did" → demo MomRise finding a recipe. Native to the platform, leverages someone else's reach.

After these 4: check TikTok analytics → which got watch-through above 65%? That's the format signal. Make more of that.

What NOT to do

  • Don't save an IG Reel from inside IG, then re-upload to TikTok. IG watermark = downranked.
  • Don't post 7x/week forcing it. Median per-post views drop above 5/week.
  • Don't shoot studio-style B-roll with stock music. Reads as "ad" — penalty under the Reali-TEA pattern.
  • Don't drag external music (Epidemic Sound, etc.) into CapCut and bake it into the master. Use platform-native audio at upload time.

Pin batch workflow

The recipe pin generator is your friend.

Use this URL

momrise.app/admin/pin-generator.html

Two tabs in your browser:

  • Tab 1: Pinterest, logged in as MomRise (shared creds)
  • Tab 2: pin generator, signed in with your Google account (you're allowlisted)

Per-pin flow

  1. Pick a board from the dropdown — tags the URL with the right tracking
  2. Find a recipe (search "chicken" to narrow, or scroll the corpus)
  3. Click the red Pin it button on the recipe card
  4. Pinterest popup opens with image + URL + description pre-filled
  5. Pick the matching board on Pinterest, hit Save
  6. Back to pin generator, next recipe — different board if it fits a different category

About 10-15 seconds per pin. 60 recipes = ~15-20 min total.

Important: destination is the App Store

Pinterest's spam classifier blocks new momrise.app domains. We're using the App Store destination on pins until that block lifts (~6 months from domain age).

The pin generator defaults to App Store. Leave it.

Don't manually paste links to momrise.app on Pinterest in the meantime — they'll get flagged as spam. Use the pin generator for everything.

Pace yourself

Pin no more than 10-15/day. Pinterest's anti-spam likes a human cadence; rapid-fire pinning on a new account looks like marketing automation. 5-10 seconds between pins reads as natural.
