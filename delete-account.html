Delete Your Account

MomRise, by MomRise (momrise.app)

You can delete your MomRise account and its associated data at any time, either directly in the app or by emailing us.

Option 1: Delete your account in the app

  1. Open the MomRise app and sign in.
  2. Go to your Profile.
  3. Choose Delete Account and confirm.

Your account and associated data are deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

Email privacy@momrise.app from the email address associated with your MomRise account, with the subject line "Delete my account". We will process your request within 30 days and confirm by reply.

Delete some of your data without deleting your account

You don't have to delete your whole account to remove data:

  • In the app: child profiles, meal plans, recipes, calendar events, milestones, routines, and to-dos can each be deleted individually from their own screens. Deleting an item removes it from our servers.
  • By email: to request deletion of specific data (for example, one child's records) while keeping your account, email privacy@momrise.app with the subject line "Delete my data" and describe what you'd like removed. We will process your request within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What gets deleted

  • Your account credentials (email, name, sign-in identifiers)
  • All child profiles you created (names/nicknames, birthdates, milestones, learning-path progress)
  • Meal plans, calendar events, activities, and app preferences

What may be retained

  • Subscription records: purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play; billing records are retained by those platforms under their policies. Cancel any active subscription in your device's subscription settings — deleting your account does not cancel a subscription.
  • Legal and accounting records we are required to keep, retained only as long as required.

Questions? Contact us at privacy@momrise.app. See also our Privacy Policy.
