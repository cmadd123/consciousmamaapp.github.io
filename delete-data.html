Delete Your Data

MomRise, by MomRise (momrise.app)

You can delete some or all of your MomRise data without deleting your account.

Option 1: Delete data in the app

Content you create can be deleted individually from its own screen, including:

  • Child profiles
  • Meal plans, recipes, and favorite meals
  • Calendar events, to-dos, and routines
  • Milestones and learning-path progress

Deleting an item removes it from our servers.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

To request deletion of specific data (for example, one child's records) while keeping your account, email privacy@momrise.app from the email address associated with your MomRise account, with the subject line "Delete my data", and describe what you'd like removed. We will process your request within 30 days and confirm by reply.

Want to delete everything?

To delete your entire account and all associated data, see Delete Your Account.

Questions? Contact us at privacy@momrise.app. See also our Privacy Policy.
