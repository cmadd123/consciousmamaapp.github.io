🚀 Just want to post — what do I do?

Where do I get content to use?

The MomRise Creator Library: momrise.app/creator/library/

All the captions, app demo clips, B-roll, and brand graphics live there. Caption snippets have copy buttons. Videos play inline — tap and watch right on the page, then download what you want.

What do I write in the caption?

Copy this, replace [CODE]:

#ad Affiliate of @momrise — code [CODE] supports my channel (free for you). [your own thought / story / hook]

The first line is required (FTC). Everything else is your voice.

About the code: using your code doesn't change anything for the person typing it in — the 7-day free trial is universal and there's no discount. The code is pure attribution: when someone subscribes after entering it, that's how MomRise knows your 50% rev share kicks in. Frame it honestly. Don't promise a discount or "free trial via code" — the trial is universal regardless.

What goes on the video?

For Reels, you need text on-screen that says "Paid partnership · #ad". It needs to be visible the WHOLE time, not just at the start. Easiest: small text in the top-right corner of the video.

How long should the Reel be?

15–30 seconds usually wins. Go up to 60 if the recipe needs it.

Do I need to film something fancy?

No. The library has app B-roll you can splice in. Your face + voiceover + one of the library clips is enough. Phone-shot is great.

🛠️ One-time setup 10 min · once

How do I turn on Paid Partnership for MomRise?

  1. Open Instagram → Settings → Creator → Branded Content
  2. "Approved business partners" → Add → search @momrise → invite
  3. We approve from our end (we'll DM you when done)
  4. Now when you post, you can toggle "Paid Partnership" → tag @momrise

This is required by Instagram any time you're compensated in any form, including rev share. One-time. Don't skip it.

I'm on a personal Instagram account — can I still do this?

You need to switch to a Business or Creator account first. It's free, takes 2 minutes, no follower drop. Settings → Account → "Switch to Professional Account."

If you're not willing to switch, you can still post about MomRise — but you can't tag Paid Partnership or do Collab posts. We'd strongly suggest switching.

How do I do a Collab post?

  1. Compose your post (Reel, Feed post, or carousel — NOT Stories)
  2. On the screen where you tag people, look for "Invite Collaborator"
  3. Search @momrise, tap to invite
  4. Hit Share
Critical: do this BEFORE you publish, NOT after. After you publish, the post starts showing in your audience first — adding us later doesn't get us in front of our audience early. The whole point of Collab is the algorithmic push to both audiences at once; that only happens if we're invited before publish.

What if I forgot to add MomRise as Collab before publishing?

The post stays great in your audience. We just can't get the joint distribution. Two fallbacks:

  • DM us the post — we'll repost to our feed and tag you (small bump, but better than nothing)
  • For your next post, set a reminder to add Collab before hitting Share

📊 What posts perform best?

What format should I shoot?

These three are the highest performers for food/parenting content:

  • "What I Feed My Kids This Week" — carousel or Reel, 5–7 meals
  • "Cook With Me" — full POV of one recipe, casual voiceover
  • "Pulling a Recipe from TikTok into MomRise" — your phone, 30s, shows the share extension working

Pull the matching app B-roll from the library, splice it where it helps.

Should I use trending audio or my own voice?

Voiceover usually outperforms trending audio for cooking content because it's more personal. Trending audio is fine for hook moments (the first 3s) but voiceover the rest. The library has an "Audio tracks that have worked" note if you want suggestions.

When should I post?

Whenever your audience is most active — you probably already know this better than us. If you don't, IG's Insights tab shows it: Settings → Creator → Insights → Audience → Most Active Times.

How often should I post about MomRise?

2–4 times per month is plenty. Spread it out. Don't make your feed a MomRise commercial — your audience comes for YOU. MomRise is one of the things you happen to use.

Should I host the Collab, or should MomRise host it?

You host it. Instagram pushes Collab posts into the host's audience first, and your audience is the bigger pool. So you compose the post in your account, invite @momrise, we accept.

The only exception: if MomRise produces a custom Reel for you — that's the Tier 2 thing below — Haley will tell you how to handle it when she sends the file.

💰 How does the money work?

How do I earn?

Every yearly subscription bought using your code gives you 50% of the gross (after Apple/Stripe processing). Recurring renewals count too as long as the customer stays subscribed.

When do I get paid?

Monthly payouts on the 1st, via Stripe to your bank. We hold each earning for 30 days from when it was credited (Stripe industry standard — covers the refund window). Minimum payout: $25. Anything below rolls to next month.

How do I see my earnings?

Sign in at momrise.app/creator/ with the email tied to your creator profile. Your dashboard shows pending balance, paid balance, and a breakdown by transaction.

How do I connect my bank account for payouts?

On the creator dashboard: tap "Connect your bank." This sends you through Stripe's secure onboarding (it's not us collecting your bank info — Stripe handles it). Takes about 5 minutes.

⚖️ The FTC stuff Required reading · 5 min

Why do I need #ad AND Paid Partnership AND on-screen text?

The FTC requires disclosure to be clear and conspicuous. Instagram's Paid Partnership label is for transparency, but it's not enough on its own per FTC rules. Together they cover both Instagram's rules and the FTC's:

  • Paid Partnership label → Instagram's requirement
  • #ad at start of caption → FTC's caption requirement
  • On-screen text overlay → FTC's video requirement (visual endorsements need visual disclosures)

You need all three for paid content (including rev-share). Skip any one and you're exposed.

What language is safe? What should I avoid?

What happens if I forget the disclosure on one post?

Fix it: edit the caption to add #ad Affiliate of @momrise at the start. Add the on-screen text in a Story re-share if you can't re-export the Reel. For Reels you've published already without overlay — leave them up but fix going forward. We'd rather you correct than panic-delete.

Are MomRise and I both responsible?

Yes. The FTC has gone after both creators and brands when disclosure fails. We protect each other by making sure the disclosure is in every post. That's why the disclosure is non-negotiable, not a stylistic preference.

🆘 Other edge cases

Can I post about MomRise on Stories?

Yes, but Stories don't support Collab posts (it's a real Instagram limitation, not us being picky). Disclosure rules still apply — add a text sticker saying #ad or Paid partnership to any Story featuring MomRise.

Can I edit my caption after I've posted?

Yes. IG lets you edit captions any time. If you forgot the disclosure, edit immediately. The Collab tag itself usually can't be added after publish (and even if it could, the algorithm has already started — see Collab question above).

Can I share MomRise on TikTok / YouTube / Pinterest too?

Yes, encouraged. Same rules: #ad Affiliate of @momrise — code [CODE] at the start of your caption/description on every platform. Add the same on-screen text overlay on any video.

My code isn't working — what do I do?

DM @momrise or email collinjmaddox@gmail.com — we'll check the configuration and resend if needed. Usually a typo issue or the code wasn't activated yet.

How do I leave the program?

DM us, no hard feelings. Your earned payouts get processed normally through the next payout cycle. We deactivate the code so no future charges run through it.
