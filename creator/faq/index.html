More questions

How do I set up Paid Partnership tagging? (one-time)
  1. Instagram → Settings → Creator → Branded Content
  2. "Approved business partners" → Add → search @momrise → invite
  3. We approve from our end (we'll DM you when done)
  4. Done — you'll see "Paid Partnership" as a toggle when composing any post.

Required by Instagram any time you're compensated (including rev share). One-time.

I'm on a personal Instagram — can I still do this?

You need to switch to a Business or Creator account. Free, 2 minutes, no follower drop. Settings → Account → "Switch to Professional Account."

If you don't switch, you can still post about MomRise but you can't tag Paid Partnership or do Collab posts. Strongly suggest switching.

How do I do a Collab post with @momrise?
  1. Compose your post (Reel, Feed post, or carousel — NOT Stories)
  2. On the "Tag people" screen, find "Invite Collaborator"
  3. Search @momrise, invite
  4. Hit Share
Critical: invite BEFORE you publish, not after. Adding the Collab after publish doesn't get us into your audience early — that's the whole point.
What if I forgot to add MomRise as Collab before publishing?

The post stays great in your audience. We just can't get the joint distribution. Two fallbacks:

  • DM us the post — we'll repost to our feed and tag you
  • Next post: set a reminder to invite Collab BEFORE hitting Share
About the code — does it give my followers anything?

No. The 7-day free trial is universal and there's no discount. Your code is pure attribution: when someone subscribes after entering it, that's how we know your 50% rev share kicks in.

Frame it honestly. Don't promise a discount or "free trial via code" — the trial is universal.

How does the money actually work?
  • Earn: 50% of every yearly subscription bought after using your code (gross, after Apple/Stripe processing). Renewals count.
  • Get paid: Monthly on the 1st, via Stripe to your bank.
  • Minimum payout: $25. Below that rolls to next month.
  • 30-day hold: Each earning is held 30 days before payout (refund window — Stripe standard).

See balance + history on your dashboard.

What format performs best? Reels vs. Stories vs. Carousels?

Three formats, three jobs:

  • Reels reach non-followers. Your launch post should be a Reel.
  • Stories convert your existing followers (link sticker, code on screen).
  • Carousels earn saves and the algorithm re-shows them for weeks.

Full strategy is in the Playbook — 3-touch arc, sample 3-week schedule, what makes a launch Reel work. The TL;DR: post 2-4 times per month, mix formats, lead with a high-pressure moment not an announcement.

FTC rules — the full version

The three things on the cheat sheet above (#ad opener, on-screen overlay, Paid Partnership label) are all required because Instagram and the FTC have different requirements:

  • Paid Partnership label → Instagram's requirement
  • #ad at start of caption → FTC's caption requirement
  • On-screen text overlay → FTC's video requirement (visual endorsements need visual disclosures)

Skip any one and we're both exposed. The FTC has gone after both creators and brands when disclosure fails.

Safe disclosure words: #ad · Affiliate · Partner · Paid partnership · Sponsored

Avoid alone: Ambassador (only with #ad) · plain text with no disclosure

If you forgot disclosure on a past post: edit the caption to add #ad Affiliate of @momrise at the start. Don't delete — fix and move on.

Edge cases — Stories, TikTok, code issues, leaving the program

Stories: yes, post away. No Collab tag (Stories don't support it). Add a text sticker saying #ad or Paid partnership.

Editing captions after posting: yes, IG allows. If you forgot disclosure, edit immediately.

TikTok / YouTube / Pinterest: encouraged. Same rules — #ad Affiliate of @momrise — code [CODE] at the start, plus on-screen text for video.

Code not working: DM @momrise or email support@momrise.app.

Leaving the program: DM us. Earned payouts process normally; we deactivate the code so no future charges run through it.
