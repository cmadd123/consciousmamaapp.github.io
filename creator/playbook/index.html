Your first post sets the campaign's velocity

The launch Reel is the highest-leverage post in the arc. Get it right or the next two underperform.

  • Warm up first. Drop 2-3 normal posts in your voice the week before the launch Reel lands. Algorithms penalize sudden sponsored posts on quiet accounts; audiences scroll faster on them.
  • First touch is a Reel, not a Story. Reels can reach non-followers — biggest possible audience pull. Stories come later in the arc.
  • Frame it as a moment, not an announcement. Bad: "I partnered with @momrise!" Better: "Here's how I planned 4 schedules this week in 8 minutes."
  • First 2 hours decide everything. Post when your audience is most active (Insights → "When followers are most active"), reply to every comment, share to your own Story within the first hour. Don't edit or delete after posting — kills algorithm trust.
  • Reference a specific moment — Sunday-night planning marathon, mid-week schedule pile-up, 5pm pivot. Generic "motherhood is busy" gives weeks 2 and 3 nowhere to refer back to.

Five quick filming rules

  • Window light, not ring light. Face the window.
  • Phone at chest height. Eye-level reads as conversation; over-head reads as ad.
  • Hook in the first 1.5 seconds. Don't set up. Open with the pressure moment — the Sunday-night planning marathon, the mid-week schedule stack, the 5pm pivot.
  • Show the app screen for ≥2 seconds. Screen recording over your face cam works great.
  • End with the code + a question. "Code [YOURCODE]. If you try it, tell me what you made for dinner." Replies boost the algo.

FTC disclosure: use Instagram's branded content tool (Settings → Business → Branded content — it's a toggle), AND put #ad in the first 1-3 lines of the caption. Hidden disclosures hurt trust without protecting you legally. FTC guidance: prominent helps, buried hurts.

Things that quietly kill conversions

  • Generic enthusiasm — "I love this app!!" with no specific moment.
  • One-and-done — posting once and hoping audiences remember.
  • Falling-apart framing — your audience trusts capable moms, not victims. Show the pressure, not the breakdown.
  • Hiding the disclosure — hurts trust without legal cover.
  • Polished commercial vibes — the 10× UGC stat is real.
  • Promoting competing parenting apps in the same window.
  • Telling people the price — let the paywall handle it.
  • Sharing your link without your code — codes survive copy-paste.
