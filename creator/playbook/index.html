Each format has a job

Reels
Reach — non-followers find you. Open with the emotional hook in 1.5 sec.
Stories
Conversion — link sticker, code on screen, 3-frame arc to followers only.
Carousels
Saves & long-tail — algorithm reshows them. List/educational hooks.

Skip single static posts — they're functionally dead for app promo. Reels have 2× the reach of carousels; carousels have ~12% higher engagement; Stories convert because they're warm-audience only.

Sources: Metricool x HypeAuditor 2025 Instagram Content Playbook; CreatorsJet 10,000-post study.

Five quick filming rules

  • Window light, not ring light. Face the window.
  • Phone at chest height. Eye-level reads as conversation; over-head reads as ad.
  • Hook in the first 1.5 seconds. Don't set up. Start with the meltdown.
  • Show the app screen for ≥2 seconds. Screen recording over your face cam works great.
  • End with the code + a question. "Code [YOURCODE]. If you try it, tell me what you made for dinner." Replies boost the algo.

FTC disclosure: use Instagram's branded content tool (Settings → Business → Branded content — it's a toggle), AND put #ad in the first 1-3 lines of the caption. Hidden disclosures hurt trust without protecting you legally. FTC says prominent helps, buried hurts.

Things that quietly kill conversions

  • Generic enthusiasm — "I love this app!!" with no specific moment.
  • One-and-done — posting once and hoping audiences remember.
  • Hiding the disclosure — hurts trust without legal cover.
  • Polished commercial vibes — the 10× UGC stat is real.
  • Promoting competing parenting apps in the same window.
  • Telling people the price — let the paywall handle it.
  • Sharing your link without your code — codes survive copy-paste.
