How to Get Your Recipes Found & Cited by AI (ChatGPT, Google AI)

Parents increasingly ask AI what to cook — here's how to become a source it quotes.

Short answer

AI answer engines quote sources that are clearly structured, answer-first, and machine-readable. To get cited, publish your recipes on a real web page (not just in-app), add Recipe and FAQ structured data (schema.org), lead with a direct answer, and make sure AI crawlers are allowed. Most social-only creators are invisible to AI because their content lives inside apps — putting it on an AEO-optimized page is the unlock, and it's exactly what MomRise does for its creators automatically.

Why social-only creators are invisible to AI

ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews pull from crawlable web pages with clear structure. Content locked inside Instagram or TikTok is hard for them to read and rarely cited. If your recipes only live on social, you're missing the fastest-growing discovery channel in cooking — parents asking AI "what can I make with chicken and rice tonight?"

What makes a page AI-citable

  • Answer-first: put the direct answer at the top, before the story.
  • Structured data: Recipe and FAQ schema so machines can parse ingredients, steps, and times.
  • Real, crawlable pages: each recipe on its own indexable URL.
  • Crawlers allowed: a robots file that welcomes AI bots (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, etc.).
  • Freshness: updated, submitted for fast indexing (sitemaps, IndexNow).

Frequently asked questions

Can AI really drive traffic to recipe creators?

Yes — a growing share of recipe and meal-idea searches now happen through AI assistants and AI Overviews, and being a cited source sends high-intent visitors who are actively deciding what to cook.

Do I need my own website to be cited by AI?

You need your content on crawlable, structured web pages — which can be your own site or a platform like MomRise that publishes AEO-optimized recipe pages for you.

What is Recipe schema and why does it matter?

Recipe schema is structured data that labels your ingredients, steps, times, and yield so search engines and AI can read them precisely. It makes your content eligible for rich results and much easier for AI to quote accurately.

How is this different from regular SEO?

Regular SEO optimizes for ranking in a list of blue links. AEO (answer engine optimization) optimizes to be the quoted answer inside AI results — related, but it leans harder on structure, clarity, and being answer-first.
