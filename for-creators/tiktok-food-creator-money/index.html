How Food Creators Make Money on TikTok in 2026

The Creator Rewards program has a 10k gate — here's how to earn before and after you hit it.

Short answer

TikTok's own Creator Rewards Program only pays creators with at least 10,000 followers and 100,000 video views in the last 30 days, so most small food creators earn elsewhere first: brand deals, affiliate links (including TikTok Shop), UGC, digital products, and rev-share programs. TikTok is unmatched for fast reach, so the smart play is to grow with recipe content while stacking income streams that don't require the 10k gate.

TikTok's native monetization (and its catch)

The Creator Rewards Program pays for original videos over ~1 minute, but you must have 10,000+ followers and 100,000 video views in the past 30 days (and be 18+). Payouts scale with qualified views. Great once you qualify — but it shouldn't be your only plan, because reach on TikTok is volatile month to month.

How to earn before (and alongside) that

  • Affiliate & TikTok Shop — tag products in videos and earn commission; food/kitchen converts well.
  • Brand deals — TikTok's discovery means small accounts can land collaborations quickly with a strong niche.
  • UGC — sell recipe videos to brands for their own ads; follower count doesn't matter.
  • Digital products — meal plans, ebooks linked in bio.
  • Rev-share programs — recurring income from your audience subscribing to an app via your code.

Play to TikTok's strengths

  • Hook in the first 2 seconds; show the finished dish immediately, then the how-to.
  • Batch-film — TikTok rewards volume and consistency.
  • Repurpose winners to Instagram Reels and Pinterest so one video earns on multiple platforms.
  • Put a link hub in your bio so every viral video can convert to income.

Frequently asked questions

How many followers do you need to get paid by TikTok?

TikTok's Creator Rewards Program requires at least 10,000 followers plus 100,000 video views in the last 30 days. Below that, creators earn through brand deals, affiliate/TikTok Shop, UGC, digital products, and rev-share programs instead.

Does TikTok pay well for food videos?

Native Creator Rewards payouts per view are modest and fluctuate, so most creators treat them as a bonus on top of brand deals, affiliate commissions, and other streams that pay more reliably.

Can you make money on TikTok with a small following?

Yes — affiliate links, TikTok Shop, UGC, and rev-share programs all work below 10k followers. TikTok's reach also means a single video can win you new customers regardless of your follower count.

What's the fastest way to start earning on TikTok?

Affiliate links / TikTok Shop and a rev-share program are the quickest to set up — you can add them today and earn from your next video, without waiting to hit a follower threshold.
