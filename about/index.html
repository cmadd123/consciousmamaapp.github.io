About MomRise

MomRise is a family meal-planning and parenting app. Its core is a weekly meal planner built around kids: plan the week's meals, get an automatic grocery list, and import recipes you find online. Alongside it, MomRise tracks milestones, suggests age-appropriate activities, and keeps the family calendar and routines in one place.

We also publish free guides on this site — answers to common feeding and parenting questions, side-by-side comparisons, and a meal idea generator — because the questions parents bring to a meal planner ("how much should my toddler eat?", "what do I make for a picky eater?") deserve clear, sourced answers.

Our editorial standards

Our guides are general information for parents, not medical advice. This is how we make them:

  • Sourced from authoritative guidance. Health, nutrition, safety and development claims are based on public guidance from organizations such as the American Academy of Pediatrics (HealthyChildren.org), the CDC, the NHS, the WHO and MedlinePlus, or on peer-reviewed research. Every guide lists its sources, and claims link to them inline.
  • Checked against the source. We read each cited source and confirm it supports the claim. When the evidence is mixed or a source doesn't support a common belief, we say so rather than overstate it.
  • How guides are written. Guides are drafted with the help of AI writing tools, then checked claim-by-claim against the cited sources before publishing. We don't publish a claim we can't source.
  • Medical review. When a guide has been reviewed by a credentialed professional (for example a pediatrician, registered dietitian or pediatric nurse), it shows a "Medically reviewed by" line with their name and credentials. Guides without that line have not yet been professionally reviewed.
  • Dated and kept current. Every guide shows when it was published and last updated. We revisit guides when the underlying guidance changes.
  • Independent. Comparison pages cover methods and product categories, not brands that pay us, and we don't accept payment to change what a guide says.
  • Your pediatrician comes first. Every child is different. Our guides point you to your pediatrician wherever individual advice matters, and you should always follow their guidance over ours.

About the recipes

Recipes in MomRise and on this site are curated from food creators around the web. Each recipe page credits and links its original creator. If you're a creator and want a recipe credited differently or removed, email dmca@momrise.app (see our DMCA policy).

Corrections

Spotted something wrong, out of date, or unclear? Email hi@momrise.app with the page link. We review every report, fix confirmed errors promptly, and update the page's "Updated" date when we do.
